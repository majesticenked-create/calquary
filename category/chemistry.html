Chemistry Calculators

Reaction kinetics, yields, and solution mixing.

Reaction rates, percent yields, and solution mixtures follow a small set of standard relationships - the Arrhenius equation, the percent-yield ratio, alligation, and the Debye-Hückel limiting law. These tools apply them to your own measurements, with units and assumptions spelled out, so you can check a result instead of guessing.
