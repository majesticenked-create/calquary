Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate running pace?

Divide your total time by your total distance. For a 5K in 28 minutes, pace per mile = 28 ÷ 3.1 ≈ 9:02 per mile.

How do I convert pace to speed?

Divide 60 by your pace in minutes per mile (or km) to get mph (or km/h). A 9:00/mile pace is 60 ÷ 9 ≈ 6.7 mph.

How do I use pace to predict a race finish time?

Multiply your per-mile or per-km pace by the total race distance - if your training pace is 9:00/mile, a 13.1-mile half marathon at that same pace would finish in roughly 1:58:00, though most runners' actual race pace differs somewhat from training pace.
