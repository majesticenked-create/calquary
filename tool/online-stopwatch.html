Frequently asked questions

How precise is this stopwatch?

It displays to a tenth of a second, updated continuously in your browser - accurate enough for everyday timing, though very high-precision athletic timing typically needs dedicated hardware.

What does the Lap button do?

It records a split time without stopping the overall clock - each lap shows both the time since the previous lap and the total elapsed time, listed most recent first.

Does the stopwatch keep running if I switch browser tabs?

Yes - it keeps timing in the background as long as this tab stays open, even if it's not the active tab. Closing the tab or browser stops it.

Can I pause and resume instead of stopping completely?

Yes - the Start button toggles to "Stop" while running; clicking it again pauses the clock, and clicking "Resume" continues from where it left off. Use Reset to clear the time back to zero.
