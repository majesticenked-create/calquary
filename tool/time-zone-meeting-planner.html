Time Zone Meeting Planner

Enter a date and time in your zone, then see the equivalent local time in three other zones at a glance.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the Time Zone Converter?

The Time Zone Converter shows one time converted between two zones. This tool shows a single meeting time across three zones simultaneously, useful for scheduling a call with people in different locations at once.

Does this handle daylight saving time correctly?

Yes - it applies each zone's actual observed DST rules for the specific date you enter, so the displayed times are correct even during DST transition periods.

Can I use this for more than 3 time zones?

This tool shows your zone plus two others at once. For additional zones, use the Time Zone Converter to check each one individually against your base time.

Why do the day names sometimes differ between zones?

A meeting late at night in one zone can fall on the next calendar day in a zone further east, or the previous day in a zone further west - the weekday shown for each attendee accounts for this correctly.
