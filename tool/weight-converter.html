Frequently asked questions

How many kilograms is 150 pounds?

150 lb equals about 68.04 kg. To convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.453592.

What's the difference between weight and mass?

In everyday use they're treated as the same - this converter, like most scales and recipes, converts weight units (kg, lb, oz, g) directly without distinguishing mass from gravitational weight, which is accurate for anything happening on Earth's surface.

Why do some scales show a slightly different number than an exact conversion?

Most bathroom and kitchen scales round to the nearest whole unit or a fixed decimal place for readability, so a small rounding gap between the scale's display and an exact mathematical conversion is normal and doesn't indicate an error in either one.

How many grams are in an ounce?

One ounce equals approximately 28.35 grams. This is the standard conversion used for cooking, postal weights, and most everyday US customary-to-metric weight conversions.

How many pounds is 200 grams?

About 0.441 pounds - multiply grams by 0.00220462, or divide by 453.592 (grams per pound). 200g is a relatively small weight, well under half a pound.
