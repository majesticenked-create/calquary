Frequently asked questions

Does this word counter save my text?

No - the count runs entirely in your browser and nothing is sent or stored.

Does this count words the same way Microsoft Word does?

Very close, but not always identical - this counter splits on whitespace, which is how most word processors count too, but edge cases like hyphenated words or numbers can be tallied slightly differently between tools.

What counts as a 'sentence' in the sentence count?

Text ending in a period, question mark, or exclamation point is counted as one sentence - abbreviations with periods (like 'Dr.' or 'e.g.') can occasionally inflate the count slightly, since the tool can't always distinguish a sentence-ending period from an abbreviation's period.
