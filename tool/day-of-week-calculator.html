Frequently asked questions

What day of the week was January 1, 2000?

Saturday. The new millennium began on a Saturday.

How is the day of the week calculated for any date?

It follows the standard Gregorian calendar's repeating 7-day cycle, accounting for leap years - the same logic your phone or computer's calendar app uses internally.
