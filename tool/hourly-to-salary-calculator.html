Hourly to Salary Calculator

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to an annual salary?

Multiply hourly wage by hours worked per week, then by weeks worked per year. At full-time hours (40/week, 52 weeks), that's hourly × 2,080. A $19/hour wage works out to $19 × 2,080 = $39,520 per year.

Why 2,080 hours in a work year?

40 hours/week × 52 weeks/year = 2,080 hours - the standard full-time work-year figure used for salary conversions, assuming no unpaid time off. If you take unpaid vacation or have fewer working weeks, use a lower "weeks worked per year" figure for a more accurate estimate.

Does this account for overtime pay?

No - this calculator assumes a flat hourly rate for all hours entered. If some of your hours are paid at an overtime rate (typically 1.5x for hours over 40/week in the US), calculate the regular and overtime portions separately and add them together.

$48,000 a year is how much an hour?

About $23.08/hour at standard full-time hours (2,080 hours/year): $48,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $23.08. To confirm with this calculator, adjust the hourly wage until the annual salary shown matches $48,000, or divide any annual salary by 2,080 directly for the same result.

How do I figure out my salary if I don't work exactly 52 weeks a year?

Reduce the weeks-per-year figure to match your actual working weeks - for example, if you take 2 unpaid weeks off, enter 50 instead of 52. At $25/hour, 40 hours/week: 50 weeks gives $50,000/year versus $52,000/year at the full 52 weeks.

Does part-time work convert the same way as full-time?

Yes, the same multiplication applies regardless of hours - it's just hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year. A part-time job at $20/hour for 20 hours/week, 52 weeks/year, comes out to $20,800/year, using the exact same formula as a full-time calculation.

Is 'hourly wage to yearly income' the same calculation as this tool?

Yes - that phrasing describes exactly what this calculator does: enter an hourly rate along with weekly hours and weeks worked, and it converts that into the equivalent annual figure.
