Frequently asked questions

How long are dogs pregnant?

The average canine gestation period is about 63 days from conception, though it can range from 58 to 68 days depending on the dog and litter size.

How accurate is a due date estimate from the mating date?

It's a reasonable estimate, but actual whelping dates can vary by several days - a veterinarian can confirm timing more precisely using ultrasound or progesterone testing.
