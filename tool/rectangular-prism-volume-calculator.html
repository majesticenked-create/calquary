Frequently asked questions

What's the formula for the volume of a rectangular prism?

V = l × w × h - multiply the length, width, and height together. A box that's 5 × 4 × 3 units has a volume of 60 cubic units.

Is a rectangular prism the same as a cuboid or a box?

Yes - "rectangular prism," "cuboid," and "box" (or "rectangular box") all describe the same 3D shape: six rectangular faces meeting at right angles. This calculator works for any of them, whichever term you use.

How is a rectangular prism different from a cube?

A cube is a special case of a rectangular prism where all three dimensions are equal (l = w = h). Every cube is a rectangular prism, but most rectangular prisms aren't cubes, since their length, width, and height differ.
