Normal Distribution Calculator

Enter a mean, standard deviation, and a value range to calculate the probability that a normally distributed value falls within that range.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the probability a value falls in a range for a normal distribution?

Convert both bounds to z-scores (z = (x − mean) / SD), look up (or calculate) the cumulative probability for each z-score, then subtract: P(lower < X < upper) = CDF(z_upper) − CDF(z_lower). This calculator does that conversion automatically.

What's the probability within 1, 2, or 3 standard deviations of the mean?

This follows the empirical rule (68-95-99.7 rule): about 68% of values fall within 1 standard deviation of the mean, about 95% within 2, and about 99.7% within 3 - try entering mean ± 1, 2, or 3 × standard deviation as your bounds to confirm this.

Is this the same as the Z-Score Calculator?

Related but different - the Z-Score Calculator finds the percentile below a single value. This calculator finds the probability a value falls between two bounds, which is more useful for questions like "what fraction of scores are between 85 and 115?"
