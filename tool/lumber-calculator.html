Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate board feet?

Multiply thickness in inches × width in inches × length in feet, then divide by 12. A 1.5×5.5×8 ft board (actual dimensions of a nominal 2×6×8) works out to 5.5 board feet.

Why use actual dimensions instead of nominal?

A nominal 2×6 actually measures about 1.5×5.5 inches after milling and drying - using nominal dimensions overstates board feet and your order.
