Frequently asked questions

How many acres is 1,000 square feet?

1,000 sq ft equals about 0.023 acres. To convert square feet to acres, divide by 43,560.

How big is an acre in more relatable terms?

An acre is about 43,560 sq ft - roughly the size of a standard American football field without the end zones, or about 90% of one.

How is a hectare different from an acre?

A hectare is a metric unit (10,000 square meters) commonly used outside the US, while an acre is an imperial unit (43,560 square feet) common in the US and UK - one hectare is approximately 2.47 acres.

How many square feet are in a square meter?

One square meter equals approximately 10.76 square feet. This conversion is commonly needed when comparing real estate listings or floor plans that mix metric and imperial units.

Is 'ac to ft2' the same as this converter?

Yes - "ac" and "ft2" are just shorthand for acres and square feet, which this converter handles alongside square meters and square yards.
