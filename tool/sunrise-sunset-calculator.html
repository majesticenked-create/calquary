Frequently asked questions

How is sunrise time calculated?

From the sun's position (declination and hour angle) for the given date and location, using standard solar position equations - the same underlying astronomy used by observatories and navigation, accurate to within about a minute.

Why are sunrise and sunset times different every day?

Earth's axial tilt (about 23.4°) means the sun's path across the sky shifts throughout the year as the planet orbits, changing day length and the exact rise/set times - most dramatically near the solstices and least around the equinoxes.

What does 'midnight sun' or 'polar night' mean?

Near the poles, at some times of year the sun never sets (midnight sun, in summer) or never rises (polar night, in winter) - this calculator flags those cases instead of showing a sunrise/sunset time that doesn't exist.

Does this account for daylight saving time?

Yes - times are shown in the selected city's local time zone, which automatically reflects daylight saving time if that zone observes it on the chosen date.
