Frequently asked questions

What does 'sun altitude' mean?

The angle of the sun above the horizon - 0° means the sun is right on the horizon (sunrise/sunset), 90° means directly overhead, and negative values mean the sun is below the horizon (nighttime).

What does 'azimuth' mean?

The sun's compass direction, measured in degrees clockwise from north - 0°/360° is north, 90° is east, 180° is south, and 270° is west. This tool also shows the nearest compass point (like SE or WNW) for easier reading.

What is solar noon?

The moment the sun reaches its highest point in the sky for that day (due south in the Northern Hemisphere, due north in the Southern Hemisphere) - it's rarely exactly 12:00 local clock time, since clock time is standardized across a whole time zone while solar noon depends on your exact longitude.

Why isn't solar noon at exactly 12:00?

Two reasons: your exact longitude within your time zone shifts solar noon from the zone's reference meridian, and the "equation of time" (Earth's elliptical orbit and axial tilt) shifts it by up to about 16 minutes throughout the year, even at a fixed longitude.
