Frequently asked questions

How do I add hours and minutes to a time?

Convert everything to total minutes, add them together, then convert back: for 9:00 plus 5 hours 45 minutes, that's (9×60) + (5×60+45) = 540 + 345 = 885 minutes, which is 14:45 (2:45 PM).

What happens if adding time crosses midnight?

The result wraps around to the next day - for example, 11:00 PM plus 3 hours becomes 2:00 AM, and this calculator flags that the result crosses into the following calendar day rather than showing an invalid 26:00.

Is this the same as the Time Duration Calculator?

No - the Time Duration Calculator finds the gap between two times you already know (like 9 AM and 5:30 PM). This calculator does the opposite: starting from one known time, it adds or subtracts a duration to find a new resulting time.
