Frequently asked questions

What are the first 10 digits of pi?

3.141592653 - pi is an irrational number, so its decimal digits never terminate or repeat, but the sequence is precisely known and verified to trillions of digits by mathematicians and computer scientists.

Why does pi have infinite digits?

Pi is irrational, meaning it can't be expressed as a simple fraction of two integers - a mathematical proof (first given by Johann Lambert in 1761) shows its decimal expansion never terminates or falls into a repeating pattern, unlike a fraction like 1/3 = 0.333...

Do I need 100 digits of pi for real calculations?

No - just 15-16 digits of pi (about what a standard calculator or programming language's floating-point number provides) is far more precision than needed for any real-world physical calculation, even at astronomical scales. Extra digits are mostly of interest for pure mathematics, computing benchmarks, and memorization records.

How many digits does pi have?

Infinitely many - since pi is irrational, its decimal expansion never ends or repeats. This calculator shows up to 100 of those digits; trillions more have been computed by supercomputers, though only a handful are useful for any real calculation.

Is this calculator using an approximation or the true digits of pi?

The true, mathematically correct digits - pi's digits up to any given precision are a fixed, known sequence (not an approximation), so the first 20 digits shown here will always match any other correct source.

What are the digits of pi to 5 decimal places?

3.14159 - useful as a quick, memorable level of precision for everyday estimation, such as circle area or circumference calculations.

Is pi digits calculator the same as a pi generator or pi finder?

Yes - all of these describe the same task of displaying pi's decimal expansion to a chosen number of digits, which is exactly what this tool does.
