Frequently asked questions

What is the circumference of a circle with radius 5?

About 31.42 - circumference = 2 × π × 5 ≈ 31.42.

What is the area of a circle with radius 5?

About 78.54 - area = π × 5² ≈ 78.54.

How do I find the radius from the circumference?

Divide the circumference by 2π: radius = circumference ÷ (2×π).

How do I find the radius from the area?

Divide the area by π, then take the square root: radius = √(area ÷ π).
