Hamster Age Calculator

Choose your hamster's species, enter its age in months, and see its likely life stage and a rough human-age comparison. Hamsters live briefly and differ by species, so treat the result as a broad estimate, not exact equivalence.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Frequently asked questions

How long do hamsters live?

It depends on the type. Syrian hamsters often live about 2 to 3 years, dwarf types a bit less, and Roborovski hamsters can reach about 3 years or more.

Is there a hamster-year to human-year formula?

There is no accepted exact formula. This tool gives a life stage and a range rather than a single number.

Why do I have to choose a hamster type?

Different types have different typical lifespans, which changes both the life stage and the human comparison.

How is this different from the Age Calculator?

The general Age Calculator works out a person's age from dates. This tool compares an animal's age to typical hamster lifespans.

How is this different from the Guinea Pig Age Calculator?

Guinea pigs live several years longer, so they use different lifespan assumptions and different age ranges.

Does the result tell me my hamster's health?

No. Health depends on the individual animal. Ask a veterinarian about any change in eating, movement, or behavior.
