Frequently asked questions

What week number is June 15, 2024?

Week 24 of 2024, using the ISO 8601 standard where weeks start on Monday.

Why does ISO week numbering matter?

ISO 8601 week numbers are used widely in business, manufacturing, and international scheduling because they give every week a consistent, unambiguous number that doesn't reset awkwardly mid-week at year boundaries.

Does every year have exactly 52 weeks?

No - the ISO week-numbering system means most years have 52 weeks, but years where January 1 falls on a Thursday (or it's a leap year starting on Wednesday) get a 53rd week, since ISO weeks are defined by whole Monday-to-Sunday periods within the year.
