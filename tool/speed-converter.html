Frequently asked questions

How many km/h is 60 mph?

60 mph equals about 96.56 km/h. To convert mph to km/h, divide by 0.621371 (or multiply by about 1.60934).

What's the difference between knots and mph?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour, which is slightly longer than a statute mile - 1 knot ≈ 1.15 mph. Knots are standard in marine and aviation navigation; mph is standard for road speeds in the US.

What's the difference between speed over ground and airspeed?

This converter handles simple unit conversion (mph, km/h, knots, etc.) for a single speed value; it doesn't account for wind or current effects that separate 'speed over ground' from 'airspeed' or 'water speed' in aviation and marine contexts, which require additional variables beyond unit conversion.
