Pet Sitter Rates Calculator

Enter your own sitting rates and booking details to estimate the total cost. The default numbers are editable examples, not current local rates - replace them with your sitter's actual pricing or your own planned rates.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Frequently asked questions

Are the default rates real market prices?

No. They are placeholder examples. Replace them with your sitter's actual quote or your own planned pricing.

What is the difference between drop-in and overnight pricing?

Drop-in visits are typically charged per visit, with multiple visits possible per day. Overnight stays are usually a flat rate per night regardless of how many times the sitter checks in.

How does the extra-pet fee work?

It multiplies by the number of extra pets and by the total number of visits or overnights, matching how many sitters charge an add-on per additional animal per visit.

Can I estimate a multi-week trip?

Yes. Increase the number of days, and the tool multiplies your per-day cost accordingly. It does not assume a discount for longer bookings unless you build one into your rate.

Does this include walking or medication administration separately?

Not as separate line items. Build any such add-ons into your per-visit or per-overnight rate, or use the extra-pet fee field creatively if that fits your situation better.

How is this different from the Cost of Owning a Dog Calculator?

That tool estimates ongoing costs of owning a pet, like food and routine care. This one estimates a single sitting booking's cost from a sitter's rates.
