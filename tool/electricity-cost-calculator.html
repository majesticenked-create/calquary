Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to run a 100W device for 5 hours a day?

At a typical rate of $0.15/kWh: about $0.075 per day, or roughly $2.25 over 30 days - 100W × 5 hours = 0.5 kWh per day, times $0.15.

How do I convert watts to kilowatt-hours?

Kilowatt-hours = watts ÷ 1000 × hours of use. A 1000-watt device running for 1 hour uses exactly 1 kWh - it's a measure of total energy consumed, not just power draw.

Where do I find my electricity rate?

Check your utility bill - it's usually listed as a price per kilowatt-hour (kWh), though many utilities use tiered or time-of-use rates that vary depending on how much you use or when, which this simple calculator doesn't account for.

Why does my calculated cost differ from my actual bill?

Real bills often include additional fixed charges, taxes, and tiered or time-of-use pricing that a simple flat-rate calculation doesn't capture - this tool gives a good estimate for a single device's marginal cost, not a full bill prediction.
