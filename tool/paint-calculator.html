Frequently asked questions

How much paint covers 400 sq ft?

At standard 350 sq ft per gallon coverage, one coat over 400 sq ft needs about 1.14 gallons - round up to be safe.

Should I subtract doors and windows from wall area?

For a rough estimate, no - most painters skip this since the extra paint from not subtracting covers trim work and touch-ups. For large window/door areas, subtracting them can save you from buying more than you need.

How much extra paint should I buy for touch-ups later?

Buy a little more than the calculated amount - rounding up to the next full gallon or quart covers touch-ups and accounts for uneven coverage on textured surfaces.
