Frequently asked questions

How random is this dice roller?

It uses your browser's random number generator, giving each side of each die an equal chance on every roll - fine for games, decisions, and casual use, though not certified for regulated gambling.

Can I roll dice with more than 6 sides, like a D20?

Yes - set "sides per die" to any number from 2 to 100, so this works for standard 6-sided dice, D20s and other tabletop RPG dice, or custom side counts.

How do I roll multiple dice and add them together, like 2d6?

Set "number of dice" to 2 and "sides per die" to 6 - the result shows each individual roll plus the total, which is exactly what "2d6" notation from tabletop games means.
