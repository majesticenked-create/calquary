Frequently asked questions

What's the square root of 144?

12 - since 12 × 12 = 144, making 144 a perfect square.

Why don't negative numbers have a real square root?

Any real number squared is positive (or zero), so no real number multiplied by itself can produce a negative result - negative square roots require imaginary numbers.

How do I estimate a square root without a calculator?

Find the two perfect squares your number falls between, then interpolate. For √50, it's between √49=7 and √64=8, closer to 7, so a reasonable estimate is about 7.07 - which matches the exact value.

What's the difference between a square root and a cube root?

A square root asks what number times itself equals the input; a cube root asks what number times itself three times equals the input. The square root of 27 isn't a whole number, but the cube root of 27 is exactly 3, since 3 x 3 x 3 = 27.
