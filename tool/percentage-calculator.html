Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number by hand?

Divide the percent by 100, then multiply by the number. For 20% of 150: 0.20 × 150 = 30.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole, then multiply by 100. For example, 30 is what percent of 150? 30 ÷ 150 × 100 = 20%.

How do I calculate a percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. Going from 150 to 180 is (180−150)/150×100 = 20% increase; going from 150 to 120 is (120−150)/150×100 = −20%, a 20% decrease.

How do I find the original price before a percentage discount was applied?

Divide the discounted price by (1 minus the discount as a decimal). If a $60 item is 25% off, divide 60 by 0.75 to get the original price of $80.

How do I calculate a percent of a percent, like 20% of 50%?

Convert both percentages to decimals and multiply them together, then convert back to a percentage: 20% of 50% is 0.20 × 0.50 = 0.10, or 10%. This comes up when combining rates - like a discount applied on top of an already-discounted price, or a tax rate applied to a partial amount.

What is 6/5 as a percentage?

120% - divide the numerator by the denominator (6 ÷ 5 = 1.2), then multiply by 100. A fraction greater than 1 (like 6/5) always converts to a percentage greater than 100%.

Is a percentage finder the same as this percentage calculator?

Yes - "percentage finder" just describes what this tool does: find what X percent of a number is, find what percent one number is of another, or find a percentage increase or decrease, all in one place.
