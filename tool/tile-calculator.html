Frequently asked questions

How many 12x12 tiles do I need for 100 sq ft?

With a 1/8 inch grout line and 10% waste allowance, 100 sq ft needs about 108 tiles.

Why does grout line width affect tile count?

Grout lines add space between tiles, slightly increasing each tile's effective footprint - over a large room this adds up to a few extra tiles beyond a simple area ÷ tile size calculation.

Should I buy extra tile beyond the waste allowance for future repairs?

It's a common practice to keep a few extra tiles from the same production batch/dye lot specifically for future repairs, since tile colors can vary slightly between manufacturing batches and an exact match may not be available later.

Does tile size affect how much waste allowance I should use?

Yes - larger tiles generally need a lower waste percentage since fewer cuts are required for the same area, while smaller tiles, mosaics, or diagonal layouts need a higher waste allowance because more pieces get cut and discarded around edges.
