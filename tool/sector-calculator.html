Frequently asked questions

What is the arc length of a 90° sector with radius 10?

About 15.71 - convert 90° to radians (π/2 ≈ 1.5708), then arc length = 10 × 1.5708 ≈ 15.71.

What is the area of a 90° sector with radius 10?

About 78.54 - area = 0.5 × 10² × 1.5708 (90° in radians) ≈ 78.54, which is exactly 1/4 of the full circle's area (π × 10² ≈ 314.16), matching the 90°/360° = 25% fraction.

Why does the formula need the angle in radians, not degrees?

Radians directly relate arc length to radius (an angle of 1 radian subtends an arc equal to the radius), which is what makes the simple formulas arc length = r×θ and area = 0.5×r²×θ work - degrees don't have that direct relationship, so this calculator converts for you automatically.

How is a sector different from a segment?

A sector is the full "pie slice" bounded by two radii and the arc between them. A segment is just the region between a chord (straight line connecting the two arc endpoints) and the arc itself - a segment is always smaller than its corresponding sector.
