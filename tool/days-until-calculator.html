Frequently asked questions

How is 'days until' calculated?

It's the number of calendar days between today and your chosen date, counting forward or backward from midnight.

Does this count weekends and holidays?

Yes - this counts every calendar day, including weekends and holidays. If you need business-day-only counts (excluding weekends), use the Business Days Calculator instead.
